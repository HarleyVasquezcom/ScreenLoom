ScreenLoom

A studio recorder that lives in your browser toolbar: record a tab, a window or the whole screen, with a red REC beacon on every page while the tape rolls, and a stopwatch in the popup. Stop saves a clip card with thumbnail, length and format.

Download the extension (.zip)

REC beacon

While recording, every page shows a small red REC pill — so you always know the tape is rolling, even in a different tab.

Clip cards

Each stop saves a card with a real thumbnail, mime type, date and length. The .webm downloads locally; the card metadata persists.

Local studio

Recording happens inside the popup page via getDisplayMedia + MediaRecorder. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Install (load unpacked)

  1. Download screenloom.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the folder.
  4. Open the popup, press start, choose what to share, press stop to save the clip.
